Where is Sadhana Study Hall in Nizamabad?+

2nd Floor, Yendala Towers Lane, opposite Irani Cafe, Pragathi Nagar, Nizamabad, Telangana 503003. The easy landmark is On the 2nd floor in the Neuroina Hospital building, Yendala Towers Lane.

What are the daily study hall timings?+

The study hall is open daily with flexible access, generally from 6:00 AM to 10:00 PM. Study until 11:00 PM can be arranged in advance.

Are study seats currently available?+

Yes. The hall has 50 individual cabins, with 17 currently unassigned in the latest cabin list. Please call or WhatsApp before visiting because availability can vary by study timing.

Which facilities are available?+

Facilities include individual study cabins, air conditioning, free Wi-Fi, personal power sockets, CCTV, R.O. drinking water, separate washrooms for men and women, and a terrace dining area.

How can I check the current membership fee?+

Call or WhatsApp Sadhana Study Hall for the current fee, available seat and a suitable study timing. You can also book a visit before joining.
